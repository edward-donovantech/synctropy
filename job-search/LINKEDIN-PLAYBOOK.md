## Why this matters more than the resume

For FDE/SE roles, recruiters source proactively on LinkedIn keyword search before you ever apply.
Your profile has two jobs: (1) surface in searches for "solutions architect AI," "forward deployed
engineer," "MCP"; (2) survive the 30-second click-through from your application. Right now your
resume says "AI-native builder" but if the profile still reads "transit solutions architect,"
you lose the thread.
